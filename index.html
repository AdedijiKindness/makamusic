ABOUT MAKAMUSIC

"Not here to make noise. Here to make History."

I'm Adeyemo Gad Ifeoluwa, you know me as MakaMusic. A law student at Obafemi Awolowo University. I fell in love with rap in 2019 and since then I've dropped five singles, including "SCATTER" and "JUBA" and my latest EP: "MAKA SZN" I was named OAU Street Hip Hop Artiste of the Year in 2024 and OAU Artiste of the Year in 2025.
Co-signed by both Asake and Yhemolee, that's not luck, that's work. Music isn't just a hobby to me, it's my life. I've got one more year of law school left, and then it's music, full-time, no looking back. Everyone will reckon with me when I become successful. I've been saying that since day one. I'm not here to make noise, here to make history.
